01 — Project Overview
The Brief

Men's Space is a premium barbershop based in Warsaw, Poland. The goal was to create a modern, content-rich landing page that communicates the brand's premium positioning and drives online bookings.

The project required a multilingual experience in three languages — English, Ukrainian, and Polish — catering to Warsaw's diverse international clientele.

The design direction followed a clean white-themed aesthetic with editorial typography, inspired by contemporary men's grooming brands. All content — team profiles, services, portfolio work — is presented through a cohesive visual system built entirely in vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
